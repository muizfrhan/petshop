## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, premium, and welcoming atmosphere tailored for a modern pet care destination. Balancing clinical veterinary rigor with the warmth and delight of domestic companionship, the aesthetic fuses clean modern corporate precision with soft, organic tactile shapes.

### Brand Attributes
- **Nurturing & Empathetic:** Interfaces prioritize approachability through generous internal card padding, warm accents, and friendly typography.
- **Authoritative & Professional:** A deep emerald green core anchors the identity, communicating wellness, safety, natural ingredients, and medical pedigree.
- **Delightful & Dynamic:** Coral accent triggers spark enthusiasm across conversions, rewards, alerts, and seasonal highlights without appearing chaotic or childish.

### Design Movement: Modern Organic Tactility
Drawing from modern minimalism with tactile, gentle depth:
- **Rounded Architecture:** Friendly, large radii (14px–20px) convey physical safety and softness, removing sharp digital coldness.
- **Layered Light:** Surfaces float using multi-layered, tinted ambient drops shadows rather than harsh linear strokes.
- **Editorial Cleanliness:** Plenty of negative space framed by subtle borders (`#E2E8F0` / `#1E293B`) gives premium shelf-space presence to products and services.

## Colors

The palette balances clean medical reassurance with warm companionship.

### Primary Spectrum: Deep Emerald & Botanical Green
- **Deep Emerald Green (`#0F5132`):** Communicates safety, longevity, and clinical trust. Used across dominant brand touchpoints, primary actions, and hero sections.
- **Active Forest (`#059669`):** Acts as an interactive mid-tone, active indicator, verification badge, and wellness indicator.

### Accent: Warm Coral
- **Warm Coral (`#FF6B4A`):** Evokes emotional warmth, playfulness, and immediate attention. Reserved strictly for primary retail CTAs, limited offers, notification pips, and subscription tags.

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Surface Foundations (`#F8FAFC` & `#FFFFFF`):** High clarity canvas allowing packaging photography and animal imagery to pop without visual contamination.
- **Slate Text Hierarchy (`#0F172A` / `#64748B`):** Deep slate ensures strong legibility without the harshness of pure black (`#000000`), paired with balanced secondary slate for pricing descriptors, meta details, and breadcrumbs.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans brings sculpted modernism, rounded terminal strokes, and exceptional clarity to the typography scale.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Section Headers:** High-contrast weights (`700` and `800`) with tight letter spacing create editorial prominence. Ideal for hero merchandising and veterinary lifestyle features.
- **Product & Spec Titles (`title-md` / `headline-sm`):** Slightly reduced tracking maintains compact structural integrity in multi-column product grids.
- **Body & Clinical Descriptions:** Generous line heights (1.5x minimum) optimize scanning during pet health intake, dietary guides, and ingredient transparency audits.
- **Metadata & Badges (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with widened letter spacing (`+0.06em`) for categorical clarity without screaming.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8pt geometric system enforces airy elegance throughout the shopping and booking journey.

### Grid Dynamics
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout, max-width 1360px centered, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px outer canvas padding.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 32px margins. E-commerce cards switch cleanly to 2 or 3 abreast.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 20px outer margin. Sticky interactive checkout sheets and filter drawers take precedence.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-2xs` to `space-xs`):** Used inside badges, icon-label pairs, and stepper controls.
- **Component Padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Product cards receive at least 16px to 24px of internal breathing room to let product photos detach from metadata.
- **Section Breaks (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`):** Substantial vertical cadence between category hubs, veterinarian appointment modules, and client reviews.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids harsh flat drops in favor of soft, tinted, multi-tiered organic shadows that mimic diffuse sunlight.

### Shadow Architecture (Emerald Tinted)
- **Level 1 (Card Rest / Flat Interactive):**
  `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.05)`
  Crisp, low separation providing baseline grounding over `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 2 (Hover State / Dropdown Menus):**
  `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.06)`
  Smooth physical lift signaling direct affordance.
- **Level 3 (Cart Drawer / Floating Navigation / Modals):**
  `0 20px 32px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.08)`
  Substantial, reassuring elevation creating immediate foreground focus.

### Surface Outlines
To prevent muddy separation on off-white backgrounds, all cards and layered surfaces carry a 1px composite outline:
`border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. In dark mode, this transitions to `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The form language is defined by warm, organic curvature without slipping into full pill forms for structural containers.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Pills):** `14px` border radius ensures inputs and action keys feel ergonomic under thumb or cursor.
- **Containers & Product Cards:** `18px` to `20px` border radius creates a distinct, friendly silhouette that softens high-density e-commerce layouts.
- **Hero & Promotional Banners:** `24px` to `28px` roundedness anchors large featured showcases.
- **Badges & Avatars:** Fully circular (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for pet profile pics, ingredient purity tags, and counter dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Checkout, Book Care):** Deep Emerald background (`#0F5132`), pure white text, 14px radius, 14px vertical / 24px horizontal padding. Hover elevates to `#0B3D26` with Level 2 elevation shadow.
- **Conversion Accent (Add to Cart, Flash Deals):** Warm Coral background (`#FF6B4A`), pure white text, Level 1 shadow. Hover triggers 2% scale expansion and `#E55636` background tint.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Off-white container (`#F1F5F9`) or 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) with `#0F172A` text. Focused states introduce a 3px ring in `#059669` at 25% opacity.

### Product Badges & Tags
- **Dietary / Organic Badge:** Soft Emerald tint (`#D1FAE5`) background, `#065F46` label-caps bold text, 9999px radius, 4px vertical / 10px horizontal padding.
- **Offer / Sale Badge:** Coral tint (`#FFF0ED`) background, `#FF6B4A` text, 9999px radius.
- **Veterinary Approved Tag:** Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) container with `#10B981` mini shield icon, offering high-contrast credibility.

### E-Commerce Product Card
- **Structure:** Clean white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `18px` radius, 1px subtle stroke (`#E2E8F0`), Level 1 shadow.
- **Image Container:** Balanced 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio on an off-white backdrop (`#F8FAFC`), rounded `14px`, isolating pet food, toys, or medical packaging cleanly.
- **Interactive State:** On hover, the container translates -4px on the Y axis, drops a Level 2 shadow, and reveals an instant floating quick-add Coral cart button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** 48px height, 14px radius, `#FFFFFF` background with 1.5px `#E2E8F0` border. Active/focus switches to 1.5px `#0F5132` border with a 4px soft emerald glow ring (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px box/circle, `#0F5132` fill when selected with a bold white check/dot. Unchecked states carry `#CBD5E1` strokes.

### Pet Profile Card (Specialty Component)
- Custom modular element displaying pet species, age, allergen markers, and assigned prescription plan. Encased in a `20px` radius container with an avatar bubble, status dot (e.g., "Vaccines Up to Date"), and subtle secondary slate information tags.
